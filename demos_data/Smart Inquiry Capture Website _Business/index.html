Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How quickly can I get started?

The Starter plan can be deployed in 5-7 days, Business in 10-14 days, and Professional in 21-30 days. We'll schedule a requirements call within 24 hours of your booking.

Do I need technical knowledge to use this?

Not at all! We provide a simple admin dashboard and full training. If you can use WhatsApp and email, you can manage your bookings effortlessly.

What are the WhatsApp Business API costs?

WhatsApp charges per message (typically ₹0.25-₹1.50 per message depending on volume). We'll help you set up the account and estimate costs based on your expected booking volume.

Can I integrate with my existing CRM?

Yes! The Business plan includes basic webhook integration with one CRM. The Professional plan offers full two-way sync with HubSpot, Zoho, or custom CRMs.

What happens after the support period ends?

Your system continues to work perfectly. We offer optional monthly maintenance plans starting at ₹3,000/month for updates, monitoring, and priority support.
